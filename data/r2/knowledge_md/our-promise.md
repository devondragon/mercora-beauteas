## Organic & Thoughtfully Made

BeauTeas is USDA-certified organic. Every blend is made with organic botanicals chosen to support healthy, radiant skin from within.

## Freshness

We pack our teas to preserve freshness and flavor. Store your tea in a cool, dry place and enjoy it by the best-by date on the package.

## If Something's Not Right

Every order in our closing sale is final, so we are not able to take a blend back because it wasn't to your taste. If your order arrives damaged, never turns up, or is not what you ordered, that is a different thing entirely: email info@beauteas.com with your order number and a photo, and we will make it right with a replacement or a refund.
