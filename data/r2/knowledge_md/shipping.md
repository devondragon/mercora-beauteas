**AI NOTES:** This is the official BeauTeas shipping policy for the closing sale. There is NO free shipping and NO expedited or overnight option: Standard is the only method sold. Shipping cost depends on how many boxes are in the cart, so never quote a dollar figure from this file: the exact amount is calculated at checkout. Do not guess delivery times, and do not offer international shipping.

## Where We Ship

BeauTeas ships within the United States (all 50 states). We do not offer international shipping.

## Shipping Costs

- Shipping is priced by how many boxes you order, so a larger order does not pay per-box postage. You'll see the exact cost at checkout before you pay.
- Standard shipping is the only method available during the closing sale. Expedited and overnight are no longer offered.
- There is no free shipping during the closing sale.

## Order Minimum

Orders are a minimum of 10 boxes. That keeps the remaining stock moving in full cases while we wind down, and it is why the cart asks for more boxes before it will let you check out.

## Processing & Handling

Orders are packed and shipped Monday through Friday. Orders placed before 12 PM Pacific typically ship within 1–2 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking as soon as your order is on its way.

## Estimated Delivery

Standard shipping is delivered by USPS, typically within 3–7 business days after your order ships, depending on your location. Delivery times are estimates and can vary due to carrier or weather delays.

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, we'll email you a tracking link. If you have a BeauTeas account, you can also find tracking in your order history.
