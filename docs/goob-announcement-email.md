# GOOB Announcement Email (Draft)

Markdown deliverable only. No send path is built here: the real customer
mailing list lives in Shopify, not in the fresh production database (see
`docs/cutover-status.md` / the memory note on prod data being a curated dev
copy). Whoever sends the campaign (Shopify email, Klaviyo, or wherever the
list actually lives) pastes the subject and body below into that tool.

Same voice as the approved `/thank-you` copy
(`.superpowers/sdd/2026-08-05-going-out-of-business-sale/thank-you-approved-copy.md`):
thank you first, the closing second, the practical details last. Not a
verbatim copy of that page; it is written for the shape of an email, but the
facts in it (10 box minimum, final sale, aged stock, no em-dashes) are the
same facts, on purpose.

---

## Subject line

Thank you, and some news about BeauTeas

*(Alternate, if the primary tests weaker: "We're closing BeauTeas, and wanted you to hear it from us")*

## Body

Hi there,

Before anything else: thank you. For every order, for the notes about how
your skin was doing, for telling your friends. A small tea company only
exists because people choose it on purpose, and you did that.

Now the news. After a lot of thought, we've decided to wind BeauTeas down for
good. Advertising and shipping both got a lot more expensive for a brand our
size, and rather than slowly get worse at this, we'd rather close well and
say a proper goodbye.

Here's what that means if you'd like to stock up one more time:

- Everything left is priced to clear, and once it's gone, it's gone.
- There's a 10 box minimum on orders. Mix and match Morning, Afternoon and
  Evening however you like; it all counts toward the same total.
- All sales are final. No returns or exchanges, because we won't be here to
  process them, but if your order arrives damaged or never turns up, we will
  always make that right.
- Our remaining stock has been in sealed, airtight storage for several years.
  It's still lovely to drink, just a little gentler than a fresh harvest,
  which is part of why it's priced the way it is.

You can read the whole story, including why we're closing and what to expect
from your order, here: **[beauteas.com/thank-you](https://beauteas.com/thank-you)**

Thank you for letting BeauTeas be part of your day 💕

The BeauTeas team
